{% if request.session.language == 'en' %}How the file must be organised{% else %}Como o arquivo deve estar organizado{% endif %} {% if request.session.language == 'en' %}
  • One price per row, ordered from the oldest to the most recent. If your file runs the other way, tick “the file is from the most recent to the oldest” in the preview.
  • A header is optional. A first row named Price, Close or similar is recognised; you can correct the choice in the preview.
  • Other columns are fine. A file with Date and Price works: pick the price column in the preview. Dates are never read as prices.
  • Decimal separator: a dot or a comma, with no thousands separator. 1,234.56 and 1.234,56 are refused as ambiguous instead of being guessed.
  • CSV separator: ;, , or tab, detected from the file.
  • Prices must be greater than zero, and from {{ limits.min_prices }} to {{ limits.max_prices }} of them. Empty rows inside the series are reported with their line, never dropped in silence.
  • Excel: .xlsx only. Save .xls or .xlsm as .xlsx or CSV first. Values are read as the sheet stored them; a formula is read from its saved result, and a cell without one is reported by its address.
